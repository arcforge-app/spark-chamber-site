PRIVACY · LAST UPDATED 2026-10-02

Your progress stays on your device

Spark Chamber collects nothing. It has no account, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, and any problem reports you save stay in the app's own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and no copy exists anywhere else.

  • On Linux, it's in the app's data folder in your home directory, or inside ~/snap/spark-chamber/ for the Snap version.
  • In a browser, it's in the browser's storage for this site. Clearing the site's data removes it.
  • Settings → Reset progress deletes it, and so does uninstalling the app.

What leaves your device

Only what you choose to share leaves your device. Copy progress backup (in Settings) and Report a problem copy text to your clipboard, and it goes only where you paste it. The app itself never sends anything.

App stores

The store you install from (for example the Snap Store, run by Canonical) records installs and updates under its own privacy notice. Spark Chamber receives only the store's aggregate statistics, such as install counts by country. It never receives anything that identifies you.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics, or tracking. It's hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. As with any web host, GitHub and Google see your IP address and browser details when you open a page. Their own policies apply: the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Spark Chamber is meant for students, including minors. Because it collects nothing, it has no data about anyone to disclose, sell, or delete.

Questions

Ask in the public feedback tracker (you'll need a free GitHub account). The app's About page says the same as this page. If they ever differ, the app is right and this page will be corrected.
