PRIVACY · LAST UPDATED 2026-10-02

Your progress stays on your device.

Spark Chamber collects nothing. It has no account, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and any problem reports you save are kept in the app's own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no copy anywhere else.

  • Linux: ~/.local/share/io.github.arcforge_app.spark_chamber/, or under ~/snap/spark-chamber/ for the Snap version.
  • Browser: your browser's storage for this site. Clearing the site's data removes it.
  • Settings → Reset progress, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

What leaves your device, only when you choose

Settings → Copy progress backup and Report a problem copy text to your clipboard. It goes wherever you paste it, and nowhere else. The app itself never sends anything.

App stores

The store you install from (for example the Snap Store, run by Canonical) records installs and updates under its own privacy notice. Spark Chamber receives only the store's aggregate statistics, such as install counts by country, never anything that identifies you.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web server, they receive your IP address and browser details when you open a page, under their own policies: the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Spark Chamber is meant for students, including minors. Because it collects nothing, it has no data about anyone to disclose, sell or delete.

Questions

Ask in the public feedback tracker (a free GitHub account is needed). The app's About page says the same as this page; if they ever differ, the app is the authority and this page will be corrected.
