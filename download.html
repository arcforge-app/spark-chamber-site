Before you start

  • This is Early Access, so expect rough edges. More topics are on the way.
  • Updates keep your progress, but it's still worth copying a backup from Settings now and then.
  • Found a wrong answer or a bug? Use the Report a problem button in the app.
  • Hands-on builds use only battery or USB power, at 12 V or less. Read the safety notes before you build.

Release notes

0.2.02026-10-02
  • Every problem in the eight core topics (134 in all) was solved again independently to check it.
  • The Bench, in the Workshop, lets you place and wire parts, predict a reading, and then check it with a multimeter. When you drag a part's value, the reading updates live.
  • The new worksheet calculator (Ctrl+K) handles units, complex numbers, and E-series rounding.
  • The new Ohm's law wheel finds V, I, R, or P from any two of them.
  • The new Terms page collects 64 technical terms, each with short drills.
  • Mastering a topic now takes five correct answers in a row, each harder than the last.
  • The simulator now uses adaptive time steps, so the Schmitt trigger no longer switches falsely. It also adds a guided flyback lab.

Known limit: the Bench has only a multimeter for now.
