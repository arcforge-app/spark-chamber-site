02 · REAL WORK

Design to spec. Pass review. The way professionals do it.

Work orders are real design jobs with real constraints. You choose parts from standard values, draw the schematic, simulate it, check it at tolerance corners, and pass a design review against real derating rules.

  1. Requirements
  2. Part selection from datasheet ratings
  3. Schematic capture
  4. Simulation
  5. Worst-case analysis
  6. Design review
  7. Bill of materials

Nothing explodes. A design that misses the spec fails review, with the finding written the way a reviewer would write it.
